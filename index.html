Consumption = seam length × Mc thread × seam qty. Each process has two editable special-thread colour slots (Contrast, Filament or Other). Enter each percentage separately; the remaining percentage stays on body thread. Seam qty 2–9 shows yellow, 10 or above shows red.

Paste operations from Excel

Copy from Excel and paste here. Accepted: SL, Process and Machine name only; the same with a seam length; or the complete process table (with or without its header row). Without a header the column order is SL (optional), Process, Machine, Seam length, Mc thread, Seam qty, Consumption. Empty numbers can be filled on the sheet later. SL is renumbered automatically.
